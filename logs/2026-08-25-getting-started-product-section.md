# Getting Started Product Section & Streamlined Landing Architecture

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Tags:** #ui #getting-started #onboarding #product-management #staff-pm #affirm #beginner #scale

## Context

Streamlined the primary landing view to focus directly on the core Staff B2B Product Manager positioning, headline narrative (`Software Engineering Manager → PM`), previous employer credibility (Beginner, Affirm, The Tech Interactive, CHM, UCSD), and a dedicated product-oriented **"Getting Started"** onboarding roadmap.

## Reflections

- Eliminating dense multi-stage pipeline blocks below previous employers allows visitors and executive recruiters to quickly grasp Tyler's profile and proceed immediately to a clear, actionable integration path.
- The 4-phase "Getting Started" funnel translates high-level Staff PM capabilities into tangible milestones (Discovery & Audit, Cross-Functional Alignment, Operational Leverage, and Self-Service Paved Paths) with concrete deliverables.

## Decisions

1. **Header & Previous Employers Retention:**
   - Display title `Staff B2B Product Manager` with subtitle `B2B at B2C scale` and transition headline `Software Engineering Manager → PM`.
   - Primary action buttons `Recruit Me` and `Read Resume`.
   - `TrustedPartnersBar` highlighting previous employers with verified brand logos.
2. **New "Getting Started" Product Section:**
   - `STEP 01 (Phase 01: Discovery & Audit)`: Audit Developer Friction & SLA Telemetry (Week 1–2).
   - `STEP 02 (Phase 02: Cross-Functional Alignment)`: Bridge Engineering, Design & GTM Strategy (Week 3–4).
   - `STEP 03 (Phase 03: Operational Leverage & Scaling)`: Systemize Workflows & Capacity Multiplier (Month 2+).
   - `STEP 04 (Phase 04: Self-Service Paved Paths)`: Deploy Zero-Touch Onboarding & Developer Ecosystems (Continuous).
   - Runtime Specifications & Parameters table (Target Role, Background, Location, Core Competencies).
   - Direct action CTA block (`Recruit Me (LinkedIn)`, `Email Tyler`, `Read Resume Spec`).
3. **Streamlined Footer:**
   - Minimal on-brand footer with LinkedIn, GitHub, and `/llms.txt`.
