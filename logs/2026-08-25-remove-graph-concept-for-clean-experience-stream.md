# Remove Graph Concept in Favor of Clean Experience Stream

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Tags:** #ui #cleanup #experience-stream #remove-graph #staff-pm

## Context

Completely removed the Cartesian graph, SVG coordinate planes, axes, and curve concepts from `TractionTimeline.tsx` and replaced it with a clean, structured Experience & 5-Year Trajectory stream.

## Reflections

- Stripping the mathematical graph representation eliminates visual noise and returns focus to the substance of the product achievements, GMV impact, and team leadership across each company.
- The company groups cleanly organize historical milestones (Computer History Museum, The Tech Interactive, Galvanize, Affirm, Beginner) and the 5-Year Horizon ($1T) with verified metrics and LinkedIn links.

## Decisions

1. **Removed Graph Concept:** Eliminated all SVG coordinate axes, plot formulas, and curve masks.
2. **Company-Centric Stream:** Structured experiences by company with employer logos, dates, verified GMV metrics, and ownership level badges.
3. **5-Year Horizon Group:** Maintained the forward trajectory to $1 Trillion (2027 &rarr; 2031) in a high-contrast trajectory block.
