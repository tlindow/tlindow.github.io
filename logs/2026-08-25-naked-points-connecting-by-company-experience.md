# Naked Points Connecting by Company Experience (No GMV Chart)

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Tags:** #ui #dataviz #naked-points #minimalism #company-connections #staff-pm

## Context

Preserved the interactive naked milestone points (clean circular dots, year tags, hover detail cards, and scroll-revealed connecting line) while completely removing GMV chart axes, logarithmic formulas, and chart grids.

## Reflections

- Eliminating the GMV logarithmic Y-axis while keeping the naked points arranged along straight horizontal company lines creates an uncluttered, minimalist trajectory.
- The 2017 origin point glides smoothly upward into view, and as the user scrolls, the line connects horizontally through each company's milestones, bridging to the next tier and culminating in the 5-Year Horizon ($1T).

## Decisions

1. **Naked Points Architecture:** Retained interactive circular pin dots with date tags and hover detail cards.
2. **No GMV Chart Axes:** Replaced logarithmic GMV Y-axis guidelines with clean, quiet company experience tracks.
3. **Horizontal Connections on Scroll:** Points within each employer share a straight horizontal line, connecting progressively as the user scrolls from left to right.
